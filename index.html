Privacy Policy – VolcanoWatch SIM

Effective date: 14 October 2026

1. Controller

KENTECH Engineering, Degenstrasse 13, 42651 Solingen, Germany – E-Mail: invesment2021@gmail.com

2. In short

VolcanoWatch SIM has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect your name, e-mail address, location or any other personal data, and we do not run our own servers for the app.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores a few settings locally on your device: the selected language, sound on/off and sound mix, and whether the optional “Pro” purchase is active. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Data requests to third-party services

To show live and background data, the app connects directly from your device to the following services. Like every internet connection, this transmits your IP address and technical connection data (e.g. device/browser type) to the provider. The app does not send your location, identity or any account data. Requests contain only the coordinates of the volcano you selected.

  • USGS (earthquake.usgs.gov) – recent earthquake feed.
  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) – wind and weather data for the selected volcano.
  • Amazon Web Services S3 – Terrain Tiles (elevation-tiles-prod.s3.amazonaws.com) – elevation data for the 3D close-up.

Their own privacy policies apply. Links to the Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program open in your web browser, outside the app. Fonts and the 3D library are bundled in the app and are not loaded from external servers.

5. Purchases

The optional “Pro” upgrade is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; we only learn from Google Play whether the purchase is valid. Google’s privacy policy applies to the payment process.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

The technical processing described in section 4 is necessary to provide the app’s features (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and in our legitimate interest in a working app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not store this data ourselves.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since we do not store personal data about you, most requests can be answered only with this information; contact us at the address above with any question.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always available at this address, with its effective date.
